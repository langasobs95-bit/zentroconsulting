3. Cookie policy

This Site uses a single first-party, technical cookie named lang. It is set only when you choose a language with the Site's language switcher and is used to show you that version on your next visits. It expires after 12 months, contains no personal data and is not used to identify or track you. As it is necessary to provide a service you have expressly requested, it is exempt from consent.

On your first visit, the Site also checks the language configured in your browser to show you the Spanish or English version. This check runs on your own device and stores nothing.

We do not use analytics, advertising or third-party cookies, which is why we do not display a consent banner. The only external service loaded is Google Fonts, which does not set cookies on your device.

If we add other cookies in the future (for example, to measure visits), we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent before setting them. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; if you delete the lang cookie, the Site will detect your browser language again.
